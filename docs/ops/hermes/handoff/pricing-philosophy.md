# Pricing philosophy

1. **Bands over vibes** — stay inside `approvals/pricing.md`.
2. **Sit at target** by default; use min to clear slow SKUs; max for rush/custom denser jobs.
3. **Never invent** a number when status is `hidden`/`TBD`.
4. **Digital cheaper than physical** when both exist (time on plotter is scarce).
5. **Deposits** (when Nav sets them) protect booth slots; Ink cites policy only.
6. **Promos** only from promo calendar / listed codes — no surprise discounts.
7. **Multi-shop** can differ by niche but must not race-to-bottom below global mins.
